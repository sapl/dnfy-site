<Section id="service" title="What Dinify is">

Dinify is a system for organising a family's food — not a recipe site or a food shop. It lets your family:

- keep a shared family menu of the dishes you actually cook;
- plan lunches and dinners for a week or a whole month and print the plan;
- assign who cooks each meal and mark eat-out, delivery, meal-kit and leftover days;
- keep a shared shopping list built from the plan, and track food expenses;
- do all of this by talking to the Dinify Chef — an AI assistant — in the app, by voice, in Telegram or through your own AI assistant.

Dinify does not sell food, does not place orders with restaurants, delivery or meal-kit services and is not a party to any agreement you make with them.
