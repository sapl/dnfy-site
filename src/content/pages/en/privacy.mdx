<Summary>

<Section id="who-we-are" title="Who we are">

Dinify is a family meal-planning service: a shared family menu, a weekly and monthly meal plan, a shopping list and an AI assistant — the Dinify Chef — that you talk to in a chat or by voice.

Dinify (“Dinify”, “we”, “us”) is the controller responsible for your personal data. For anything related to privacy, write to [hello@dinify.me](mailto:hello@dinify.me).

</Section>

<Section id="scope" title="What this policy covers">

This policy applies to:

- this website, [www.dinify.me](https://www.dinify.me);

<Section id="data-we-collect" title="Information we collect">

### Account information

You sign in with Google. From your Google account we receive your **name**, **email address** and **profile picture**. We store a copy of the picture as your Dinify avatar. We never receive your Google password.

### Your family profile

Dinify is built around a family. During the short onboarding chat and later, you may tell the Chef, and we store:

- the family name and its members, and who is the main cook;
- language, time zone and country (used for currency and local products);
- food preferences: favourite cuisines and dishes, foods you exclude, allergies or diets, how often you cook, eat out or order delivery;
- notes the Chef keeps about your family so it can plan “like a member of the household”;
- an optional monthly food budget.

### Content you create

- recipes in your family menu, including photos you add;
- your meal plan: dishes per day and meal, who cooks them, and eat-out, delivery, meal-kit or ready-made slots;
- your shopping list, favourite items and a short history of what you buy;
- food expenses you record: amount, category and comment.

### Conversations with the Chef

- **Messages** you send and the Chef's replies, in the app or in Telegram, are stored as your chat history.
- **Images** you send (for example a photo of a receipt or a recipe) are stored so they can be shown in your chat.
- **Voice:** when you dictate a message, the audio is sent for transcription and only the resulting text is kept. **We do not store audio recordings.**

### Telegram

If you link the Telegram bot, we store your Telegram username and chat ID so the Chef can answer you there and send your daily meal plan.

### Connected AI assistants

If you connect Dinify to ChatGPT, Claude or another assistant, we store the technical authorization for that connection (client registration and access tokens).

### Subscription and payment

Payments are handled by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers and whether your subscription is active. **We never see or store your card details.**

### Technical information

- server logs with your IP address, requests, time stamps and error details;
- aggregated AI usage per family (amount of AI processing used per day), used for plan limits;
- an authentication cookie in the web app that keeps you signed in.

### Website analytics

On this website (not in the web app) we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site: pages viewed, how you arrived (for example from a search engine or a link), approximate location (country or city), device and browser type. Google Analytics sets cookies and identifies your browser by a random ID; it doesn't tell us who you are, and Google doesn't log or store your IP address. We don't use this data for advertising.

### Emails to us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

</Section>

<Section id="sensitive-data" title="Allergies, diets and other sensitive details">

Information about allergies, intolerances or diets can reveal something about your or your family's health. Sharing it is **optional**. If you choose to tell the Chef, we use it only to plan suitable meals and shopping lists for your family, based on your explicit consent. You can ask the Chef to forget it, or remove it from your profile, at any time.

Please only share details about other family members if they are happy for you to do so, or if you are their parent or guardian.

</Section>

<Section id="ai" title="How the Dinify Chef uses AI">

The Chef is powered by large language models provided by **OpenAI**. To answer a request, we send OpenAI what is needed for that request: your message, relevant family context (for example preferences and the current plan), images you attach and, for voice input, the audio to transcribe. AI is also used to fill in recipe cards — ingredients, steps, cooking time and nutrition — from just a dish name.

- OpenAI processes this data on our behalf. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train their models.
- We do not sell your conversations or use them for advertising.
- AI answers can be wrong. Always check ingredients and allergens yourself — see our [Terms of Service](/terms#ai).
- Dinify does not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.

</Section>

<Section id="family-sharing" title="Sharing within your family">

Members of the same family share one space. Everyone in your family can see and change the family menu, meal plan, shopping list, expenses, budget and family preferences, and sees who is cooking which meal.

The family owner can remove members. A removed member moves to their own personal space; content created in the family stays with the family.

</Section>

<Section id="connected-services" title="Connected services">

- **ChatGPT, Claude and other assistants.** When you connect one of them to Dinify, that assistant can read and change your family's Dinify data on your instructions. What you say in that assistant is governed by its provider's privacy policy, not ours. You can revoke the connection at any time.
- **Telegram.** Messages you exchange with the bot also pass through Telegram and are subject to Telegram's privacy policy.
- **Meal kits, delivery and restaurants.** Planning a meal-kit, delivery or restaurant slot is just a note in your plan. We do not send your data to those services or place orders for you.

</Section>

<Section id="your-rights" title="Your rights">

Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR — you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
- correct inaccurate data (most details you can simply change by telling the Chef);
- delete your data or your whole account;
- export your data in a portable format;
- object to or restrict processing based on legitimate interest;
- withdraw consent at any time, for example for allergy information or product emails.

To use any of these rights, write to [hello@dinify.me](mailto:hello@dinify.me) from the email linked to your account. We reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

</Section>

<Section id="children" title="Children">

Accounts are for people aged 16 or older. Dinify is meant for the adults who organise a family's food; parents may add information about their children, such as their food preferences, to the family profile. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

</Section>

<Section id="cookies" title="Cookies and similar technologies">

**This website** doesn't use advertising cookies. It uses Google Analytics cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_*`, kept for up to two years) to count visits and see which pages are useful. To opt out, block cookies for this site in your browser settings or install the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). Learn more in [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). The website also remembers the language you pick in the footer — in your browser's local storage and a `NEXT_LOCALE` cookie kept for up to a year — so it opens in that language next time.

**The web app** uses one strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in (it lasts up to six months, or until you log out) and your browser's local storage for interface settings. These are needed for the Service to work and don't require consent. The web app doesn't use analytics cookies.

</Section>
